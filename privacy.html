Privacy Policy

Learn how Xyntriq handles and protects your personal information.

1. What Information We Collect

We collect information that you submit to us directly through our website, such as when you fill out a contributor application form, request a quote, or correspond with us via email. This may include your name, email address, country, and any other details you provide. We also collect basic technical data that your browser shares when you visit our site, such as IP address and browser type.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Processing and evaluating contributor applications and onboarding
  • Responding to your enquiries and providing quotes
  • Providing and improving our data services
  • Communicating with you about projects you are involved in
  • Meeting our legal and regulatory obligations

3. Sharing Your Information

Xyntriq does not sell your personal information to third parties. We share your information only as needed with service providers who help us operate our business (e.g., email service providers or form hosting services), or when required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Retention & Security

We keep your personal information only as long as is necessary for the purposes for which it was collected. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, and to ask for it to be corrected or deleted. To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@xyntriq.in.

6. General Information

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13. We may update this policy from time to time, and any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@xyntriq.in.
