Before You Write

What to share for an accurate quote

The more context you send, the tighter the scope and the quote. Here's what helps us respond fast.

2–5 real sample images or framesClass list + average objects per imageYour labeling guidelines (or we draft them)Expected volume & timelineQuality targets / acceptance criteria

Computer Vision (Images)

Send 3–5 representative images per scenario, a class list with approximate objects per image, and note challenging cases like occlusion or low light.

Video

Share 2–3 short clips (5–15s) per scenario with frame rate and duration, classes and objects per frame, and whether you need tracking or detection.

Text & NLP

Provide 50–200 raw samples if available, note document types and target languages, list entities or classes, and specify labeling units like sentence or paragraph.

Audio & Speech

Send 3–10 short clips, note speakers and accents, the transcription format you need (verbatim or normalized), and whether timestamps are required.

3D / LiDAR / Geospatial

Share 3–5 point clouds or scenes, note the sensor type, your annotation schema (cuboids or segmentation), and coordinate conventions.

POV / Egocentric Video

Provide 2–3 sample recordings, describe the capture device and mount, list the tasks to be recorded, and include consent and privacy notes.

Not sure what you need? Email us at sales@xyntriq.in with whatever you have, we'll tell you what's missing.
