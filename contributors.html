FAQ

Contributor questions

How do I get paid?

Approved work is paid out weekly.

What is a "first-person (POV)" video?

A video recorded from your own point of view using a head-mounted phone, like cooking, driving, shopping, or household tasks.

Who can apply?

We are currently accepting contributors from Latin American countries only for this opportunity.

Do I need professional equipment?

No. A compatible smartphone from our approved brands and models, plus a basic head mount, is all you need.

How do I get paid?

Weekly payouts for approved video hours. You'll receive your Contributor ID and the bank details form by email after applying.
